Usage evidence
NASA SVS states its content is in the public domain unless otherwise noted:
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/
No contrary item-specific exception was found for these map downloads.
NASA media-use and agency-mark restrictions still apply:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
The Moon item's linked PDS source data has a public-domain release, distinct
from separately copyrighted curated LROC website imagery:
https://www.lroc.im-ldi.com/about/terms

These are unchanged original files: no cropping, resizing or re-encoding.
Battery, Balanced and default Story use 1024-pixel maps; Commander Quality
uses 2048-pixel maps. The Moon tiers contain different 2019 and 2025 revisions,
not identical imagery resampled at different resolutions.

Scientific scope
Earth is a static geographic photographic composite with baked topographic
shading, not a pure quantitative albedo map, live weather or a date-specific
historic scene. Moon is a normalized, vision-adjusted visualization with polar
fill, optimized for appearance rather than quantitative science. These globe
maps do not geolocate the fictional local terrain or supply experiment values.
Original procedural fallback maps remain available when image loading fails.
