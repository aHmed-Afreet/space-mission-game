ORBITAL / MISSION LAB

Privacy & Local Saves

Last updated: November 14, 2026

ORBITAL Mission Lab is an independent educational space simulation. You can play without creating an account or entering a name or email.

What the game saves

Commander Mode keeps campaign progress, equipment, mission reports, credits, checkpoints and control and sound settings in your browser's local storage. This allows you to resume after closing the game. Strategy Mode saves your unlocked levels and best scores in this browser's local storage; the current spacecraft design and event choices remain in memory during a flight. Real Space History saves which interactive first-person stories you have completed in this browser. Story mission progress is local and not intentionally uploaded to a game server.

How data is used

Solo game data stays in your browser's local saves. No sign-in, in-game chat, microphone or camera permission is required, and the game does not run advertising or analytics scripts.

Optional Commander online multiplayer

When you explicitly create or join a private Commander crew, anonymous browser peers connect using WebRTC. The game exchanges astronaut positions, movement, assigned crew roles, equipment operations and the host's current mission state with the other players in that room. These data are not shared when you play solo.

The optional room code uses a random 12-character invitation token. Only share it with teammates you trust. The code is not a registered account or a guarantee of secrecy once shared.

PeerJS Cloud (peerjs.com) provides connection signaling and may process connection metadata such as IP addresses; WebRTC browsers or relays may also expose network connection metadata to peers. During a direct data connection, mission packets are sent between players rather than stored by an ORBITAL game account server. Do not enter identifying information into room codes.

The mission host controls the shared simulation and personal game saves remain local. Guest copies of a host's active mission are not written to the guest's individual campaign save. A room stops when its host disconnects, and some networks may block connections without a TURN relay.

Hosting and technical information

The game is served by Vercel. Hosting infrastructure may process connection information, such as IP addresses, requested pages and diagnostic logs, under its own policies. The game developer does not control hosting logs from within the app.

Deleting saved progress

Open Commander Mode → Settings → Reset campaign progress and confirm to clear the current campaign and backup in this browser. You can also clear the site's data through your browser settings. Separate legacy-format saves, if present, are not erased by this reset.

External links

History links open official NASA pages outside the game; those pages have their own privacy practices. NASA does not operate or endorse ORBITAL Mission Lab.

Safety and accuracy

ORBITAL is a game, not certified astronaut training or operational flight-planning software. Procedures, spacecraft systems and time scales are simplified for educational play.
