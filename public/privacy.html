ORBITAL / MISSION LAB

Privacy & Local Saves

Last updated: October 19, 2026

ORBITAL Mission Lab is an independent educational space simulation. You can play without creating an account or entering a name or email.

What the game saves

Commander Mode keeps campaign progress, equipment, mission reports, credits, checkpoints and control and sound settings in your browser's local storage. This allows you to resume after closing the game. Strategy Mode holds its current game state in memory.

How data is used

Game data is used to power local gameplay. This version does not include sign-in, in-game chat, advertising or analytics scripts. The app does not intentionally upload game progress to a game server.

Hosting and technical information

The game is served by Netlify. Hosting infrastructure may process connection information, such as IP addresses, requested pages and diagnostic logs, under its own policies. The game developer does not control hosting logs from within the app.

Deleting saved progress

Open Commander Mode → Settings → Reset campaign progress and confirm to clear the current campaign and backup in this browser. You can also clear the site's data through your browser settings. Separate legacy-format saves, if present, are not erased by this reset.

External links

History links open official NASA pages outside the game; those pages have their own privacy practices. NASA does not operate or endorse ORBITAL Mission Lab.

Safety and accuracy

ORBITAL is a game, not certified astronaut training or operational flight-planning software. Procedures, spacecraft systems and time scales are simplified for educational play.
